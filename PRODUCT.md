# Product contract

Verity serves developers taking over trusted-source repositories whose contents are unfamiliar.

The sole product job is to determine whether a selected target can complete its declared install, offline build, test, launch, and machine-oracle path in the current recorded environment. Verity must stop at the first observed blocker and never convert process, port, or window signals into a verified result.

Out of scope: business-purpose inference, project briefings, knowledge graphs, plugin management, delivery scoring, account login, payment, cloud upload, hosted execution, malicious-repository sandboxing, or remote attestation.

Success means a traceable run plan, an isolated snapshot, no false passes in the accepted corpus, and a tamper-evident local receipt. Missing evidence is a block, not a guess.
